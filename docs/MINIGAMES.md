# Smartest in the Room: every minigame

69 minigames. Generated from `MinigameRegistry.cs`; regenerate rather than edit by hand.
**NEW** marks the 15 added on 29 Sep 2026.

Every minigame is played in levels. Everyone gets the same level at the same moment; levels 1-2
are warm-ups where only failing puts you out, and from level 3 the slowest (or worst) player also goes out.
The rule line below is exactly what players read on the rule card.
